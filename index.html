Fluctuations in Continuum

Mean-Field Limits, Singular Interactions, Fluctuations,
and their Interplay with Machine Learning

Berlin · 19–23 July 2027

The conference

Recent years have witnessed major advances in the analysis of large interacting-particle systems and their continuum limits. These include quantitative, conditional, and uniform-in-time propagation-of-chaos estimates; mean-field limits for systems with singular or low-regularity interactions; fluctuation theories and large-deviation principles in the presence of idiosyncratic and common noise; and a deeper understanding of long-time behaviour, metastability, and phase transitions. Important progress has also been made in the study of heterogeneous, non-exchangeable systems and of particle dynamics on large graphs.

At the same time, mean-field and continuum methods have developed increasingly strong connections with machine learning. Notable examples include the training dynamics of wide neural networks, mean-field Langevin methods, and continuum descriptions of deep neural networks and transformer dynamics.

By bringing together researchers from probability, analysis, statistical mechanics, and machine learning, the conference aims to stimulate the exchange of ideas and foster further cross-fertilisation among these rapidly developing areas.

The conference is organised within the framework of the ERC Consolidator Grant FluCo.

Scope

  • Mean-field limits, propagation of chaos, and singular or low-regularity interactions
  • Fluctuations, large deviations, and common noise
  • Long-time behaviour, metastability, and phase transitions
  • Heterogeneous, non-exchangeable systems and particle dynamics on large graphs
  • Mean-field methods in machine learning: wide networks, training dynamics, transformers
